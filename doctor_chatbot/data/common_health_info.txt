# Common Health Information (Sample knowledge base for Doctor Chatbot RAG)
# This is general information only, not medical advice. Consult a doctor for diagnosis.

## Common Cold
Symptoms: runny nose, sneezing, sore throat, mild fever, cough.
General care: rest, warm fluids, saline gargle. See a doctor if fever above 101F
persists more than 3 days, breathing difficulty, or symptoms worsen after 10 days.

## Viral Fever / Flu
Symptoms: high fever, body ache, chills, headache, fatigue.
General care: rest, hydration, paracetamol as directed on the pack.
Seek urgent care for breathing difficulty, chest pain, dehydration, or fever above 103F.

## Migraine / Headache
Common triggers: stress, lack of sleep, dehydration, skipped meals, bright light.
General care: rest in a dark quiet room, hydration, regular sleep.
See a doctor for sudden severe headache, vision changes, weakness, or frequent attacks.

## Acidity / Gastritis
Symptoms: burning in chest, sour taste, bloating, stomach pain.
General care: avoid spicy/oily food, eat small frequent meals, avoid late-night eating.
See a doctor for black stools, vomiting blood, severe abdominal pain, or weight loss.

## Diabetes - General Awareness
Symptoms: frequent urination, excess thirst, fatigue, slow wound healing.
Management is lifelong and needs a doctor: blood sugar monitoring, diet, exercise,
medication as prescribed. Never start or stop diabetes medicine without a doctor.

## Hypertension - General Awareness
Often has no symptoms. Risk factors: high salt, obesity, smoking, alcohol, stress.
Management needs regular BP checks and a doctor's guidance on lifestyle and medicine.

## When to seek emergency care
Chest pain, breathing difficulty, fainting, severe bleeding, signs of stroke
(face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), severe burns, or high fever
with confusion - go to emergency immediately.
